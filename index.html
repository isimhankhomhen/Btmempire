Last updated: June 2025  ·  BTM Empire

At BTM Empire, operated by Innocent Isimhankhomhen, your privacy is something we take seriously. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our store, how we use it, and the choices you have. Please read it carefully — it's written in plain English so you actually understand it.

BTM Empire is an independent online store and affiliate marketing platform. We curate and sell products directly ("BTM Store" items) and also earn commissions on purchases made through Amazon affiliate links. Our store is operated by Innocent Isimhankhomhen.

For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at: btmempire@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways — information you provide to us directly, and information collected automatically.

Information You Provide
  • Order information: When you place an order through our BTM Store, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and shipping address to fulfil and confirm your order.
  • Payment data: We do not store your payment card details. Payments are processed securely by Flutterwave (card) or NowPayments (crypto). Only they handle sensitive payment information.
  • Communications: If you email or contact us, we retain your message and contact details to respond to you.
Information Collected Automatically
  • Browser & device data: Standard web data such as your browser type, device type, and general location (country/region) may be collected via standard hosting analytics.
  • Cookies: We use minimal, essential cookies to remember your cart and saved items during your session. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.
  • Affiliate tracking: When you click an Amazon affiliate link, Amazon may place cookies on your device per their own privacy policy. We receive a commission if you purchase, but we do not receive your personal data from Amazon.
3. How We Use Your Information
  • To process and fulfil your orders, and send order confirmation emails.
  • To contact you about your order if an issue arises (e.g. payment or shipping).
  • To improve our store — understanding what products customers are interested in.
  • To comply with legal obligations where required.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

4. Amazon Affiliate Disclosure

BTM Empire is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

Products labelled "Amazon" on our site will redirect you to Amazon.com when you click "Buy Now." Any purchase you make on Amazon after clicking our link may earn us a small commission — at no extra cost to you. Amazon operates its own privacy practices which you can review at amazon.com/privacy.

5. Data Retention

We retain order information (name, email, shipping address) for up to 3 years for record-keeping and tax compliance purposes. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, contact us and we'll action it within 30 days unless we're legally required to keep it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at btmempire@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Payments are handled by PCI-compliant third-party processors (Flutterwave / NowPayments) — we never touch your card data. However, no online transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our store is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the store after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions? Contact Us
